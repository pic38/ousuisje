WhereAmI shows your position, altitude, speed and accuracy, as reported by your device's GPS. The app translates your coordinates into a readable address and offers a search for nearby points of interest (drinking water, toilets, food…), as well as a precipitation radar.

In France, additional features appear automatically based on your detected position: a search for 2G/3G/4G/5G cell towers, department and commune identification, and a comparison of GPS altitude against IGN's reference data.

The app can work offline for some of its features once the data has been downloaded (GPS position, department/commune, cell towers, drinking water, toilets). Only the features that query an online service (address, altitude comparison, other points of interest, radar) need an active connection.

No application server of its own: no location data is collected, stored, or transmitted to a WhereAmI server. The app contacts the relevant third-party service directly (OpenStreetMap, IGN, ANFR, RainViewer), never through an intermediary.

Available in 26 languages (the 24 official languages of the European Union, Mandarin Chinese and Arabic).

Data sources: OpenStreetMap and its contributors (ODbL license), IGN (RGE ALTI, Admin Express), ANFR (open license), Open-Elevation, RainViewer.
